# Satellite Antenna Bracket: Topology Optimisation and AM

Individual report for Advanced Manufacturing (UFMF74-15-M), UWE Bristol (2025).

Looks at redesigning a satellite mounted antenna bracket using topology optimisation, and compares laser powder bed fusion (LPBF) with a hybrid additive plus machining route.

## Covered
- Technical challenges: excess structural mass, integrity under launch loads, thermal stability in orbit, manufacturability of optimised geometry
- Commercial challenges: production cost and lead times
- Industry and market drivers
- Root cause analysis and a cost and manufacturability framework for choosing the process
